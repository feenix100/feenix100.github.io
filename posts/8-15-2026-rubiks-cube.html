Human CFOP Solve Guide

1. Cross

Build the four bottom cross edges and match their side colors to the center stickers. Try to plan most or all of the cross before your first move.

2. F2L — First Two Layers

Pair each bottom-layer corner with its matching middle-layer edge, then insert the pair. Learn how the right and left triggers affect pieces instead of memorizing every case.

3. OLL — Orient Last Layer

Use two looks: first orient the top edges to form a cross, then orient the four top corners. Sune and Anti-Sune cover core corner patterns.

4. PLL — Permute Last Layer

Keep the top face oriented while moving last-layer pieces to their correct locations. Use U setup turns, execute the case, then align the final layer.

How to practice for speed

Become consistent with Cross → learn intuitive F2L → learn two-look OLL and PLL → add full PLL → add full OLL. Prioritize look-ahead and smooth turning before raw speed.

CFOP Algorithm Reference

Prime (′) means counter-clockwise; 2 means 180°. Lowercase letters are wide turns, M is the middle slice, and x/y rotate the whole cube.
